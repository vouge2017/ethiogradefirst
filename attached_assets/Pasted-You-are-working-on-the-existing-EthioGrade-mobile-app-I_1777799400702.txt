PART 2 — CORRECT PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

EthioGrade has two major modes.

Mode 1: Quick Assessment
- No class setup required.
- No student registration required.
- No long history required.
- Teacher sets answer key manually or through future Master Scan.
- Teacher grades students quickly.
- Student name/ID optional.
- Useful for one-time fast grading.
- Can export optionally.
- Minimal history.

Mode 2: Class Assessment
- Teacher creates/selects class.
- Teacher imports/registers students.
- Teacher creates assessment name.
- Teacher sets answer key manually or through future Master Scan.
- Teacher batch scans or manually grades student papers.
- App detects score, confidence, missing students, duplicate scans, and review-needed papers when scanning exists.
- Teacher reviews only exceptions.
- Results are saved, exported, and kept in history.

Important concept: Master Scan
Master Scan means:
The teacher marks the correct answers on a paper answer sheet, scans that answer sheet, the app detects the answer key, shows the detected key with confidence, and asks the teacher to confirm/edit. After confirmation, the detected key becomes the answer key for grading student papers.

Master Scan exists to avoid forcing teachers to tap every answer manually on the phone.

Do not implement real Master Scan in this task unless it already exists.
Do not create fake Master Scan.
For now, align docs, architecture, navigation labels, and roadmap.

PART 3 — THREE-LANE SCANNING STRATEGY
==================================================

Important product decision:
Do not force teachers to use a printable template from day one.
But also do not promise perfect “scan any paper” grading.

EthioGrade scanning strategy has three lanes:

Lane 1: Any Paper Assist
- Teacher can scan their existing paper.
- App may help crop/straighten/read if possible in the future.
- Teacher confirmation is required.
- Flexible but slower and lower confidence.
- Do not claim it can grade 100 students in 5 minutes.
- Future/experimental.

Lane 2: Guided Paper Layout
- Teacher can use their own paper, but follows a simple recommended layout.
- Example:
  1. A
  2. C
  3. True
  4. B
- Better accuracy than any paper.
- Does not require official EthioGrade printed template.
- Good bridge for adoption.

Lane 3: EthioGrade Printable Template
- Recommended for high-speed grading.
- Uses fixed answer sheet with alignment markers.
- Supports future Master Scan and student scan.
- Best for batch scanning and large classes.
- Required for future 50–100 students fast grading.

Product principle:
The more flexible the paper, the more teacher review is required.
The more structured the paper, the more automatic and fast grading can be.

Do not implement real scanning now unless already present.
This task is product architecture, docs, navigation alignment, dependency review, and gap reporting.
