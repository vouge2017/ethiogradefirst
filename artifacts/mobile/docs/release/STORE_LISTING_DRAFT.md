# Play Store Listing Draft — EthioGrade

## App Name
**EthioGrade**

## Short Description (≤80 characters)
Offline exam grading assistant for teachers.

## Full Description

EthioGrade helps Ethiopian teachers grade objective exams faster — without internet, without complex setup, and without requiring a class database to get started.

**How it works:**

1. Enter your answer key
2. Scan or import your students' answer sheets
3. Review detected answers and correct any uncertain ones
4. Confirm scores and export results as CSV

**Made for the classroom:**

Ethiopian classrooms face real challenges: low-end phones, unstable internet, and time-consuming manual grading. EthioGrade is built specifically for these realities. Core grading works completely offline. No account. No cloud dependency. Just you, your phone, and your papers.

**Quick Assessment Mode:**

Start grading without creating a student database. Enter your answer key, scan papers, review results, and export — that's it. Designed for teachers who need results quickly.

**OMR Detection with Teacher Control:**

EthioGrade detects bubble-sheet answers using the GradeFlow Bubble 20 v1 template. Every detection is reviewed by you before the score is finalized. Uncertain answers are flagged for your attention — the app never silently trusts a result you haven't confirmed.

**Results and Export:**

View per-paper scores, class average, and pass rate at a glance. Export results as CSV to share with administrators or import into spreadsheet software.

**Honest about limitations:**

- Best results with the GradeFlow Bubble 20 template
- Poor lighting or misaligned sheets may require manual correction
- Essay and short-answer grading is not supported in this release
- Teacher review is always required before scores are finalized

---

*EthioGrade is an independent tool for classroom use. It is not an official Ethiopian government or Ministry of Education product. Grading accuracy depends on scan quality and teacher review.*
