# Teacher Pilot Script — EthioGrade

## Purpose
This script guides a teacher through a supervised pilot session to test EthioGrade in a real classroom scenario. Use this for internal testing or early user feedback.

## Setup
- Device: Android phone (prefer a low-end device similar to what teachers use)
- Internet: Disable (airplane mode) to test offline capability
- Prepare: 5–10 sample bubble-sheet answer papers (or printed test sheets)
- Time needed: ~20 minutes

---

## Script

### Introduction (2 min)

Say to the teacher:

> "We're testing a new app called EthioGrade that helps grade objective exams faster. I'd like you to try it as if you just finished an exam with your students. Please think out loud as you go — say what you expect, what confuses you, and what feels right. There are no wrong answers."

---

### Task 1: Start a New Assessment (3 min)

**Say:** "You've just finished an exam with 20 questions. Please start a new grading session."

Watch for:
- Does the teacher find the "New" button easily?
- Do they understand "assessment title"?
- Can they select 20 questions without confusion?
- Can they fill in the answer key without help?

**Note any difficulty points.**

---

### Task 2: Scan a Paper (5 min)

**Say:** "Now scan the first student's paper."

Watch for:
- Does the teacher try camera first or gallery?
- Do they understand the permission dialog?
- Is the frame guide helpful or confusing?
- Do they understand "processing" means the app is working?

**Note any difficulty points.**

---

### Task 3: Review the Result (5 min)

**Say:** "The app has detected answers. Please review them before confirming the score."

Watch for:
- Does the teacher notice the flagged/uncertain answers?
- Do they understand how to correct an answer by tapping?
- Do they read the issue warnings?
- Do they confirm the result or leave without confirming?

**Note any difficulty points.**

---

### Task 4: Scan 2 More Papers (3 min)

**Say:** "Now scan two more papers."

Watch for:
- Is the flow from review back to scanning smooth?
- Does the paper counter make sense (Paper 2, Paper 3)?

---

### Task 5: View Results (2 min)

**Say:** "Please look at the results for this exam."

Watch for:
- Does the teacher understand class average and pass rate?
- Do they notice the pending vs. reviewed sections?

---

### Task 6: Export Results (2 min)

**Say:** "Please export the results so you can share them."

Watch for:
- Does the teacher find the Export CSV button?
- Do they understand the share sheet that appears?
- Do they know what to do with the CSV?

---

## Post-Session Questions

1. How long does it usually take you to grade 40 papers manually?
2. Would this app save you time? Where?
3. What was the most confusing part?
4. What is missing that you would need?
5. Would you use this in your next exam? Why or why not?
6. How is the phone you use compared to this test device?

---

## Data to Collect

- Time taken per paper (from scan to confirmed)
- Number of corrections made per paper
- Number of errors or confusion moments
- Teacher's confidence rating (1–5)
- Willingness to use again (Yes / Maybe / No)

---

## Notes

Please do not intervene unless the teacher is completely stuck. Record observations without guiding their choices. After the session, debrief with the questions above and document findings for the team.
