# Privacy Policy — EthioGrade

*Last updated: May 2026*

## Overview

EthioGrade ("the app", "we", "us") is an offline exam grading tool for teachers. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how it is stored.

## Data Collected

### Data stored on your device (local only)

EthioGrade stores the following data locally on your device using your phone's storage:

- **Assessment data**: Titles, answer keys, paper counts, and score results you enter
- **Grading results**: Detected and corrected answers, confidence scores, and final scores
- **Captured images**: Photos of answer sheets taken using the camera or imported from your gallery

### Data we do NOT collect

- We do not collect student names, student IDs, or any personally identifiable student information
- We do not transmit any data to external servers
- We do not require account creation or login
- We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking

## Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Camera | Capture photos of answer sheets for grading |
| Photo Library / Media | Import existing photos of answer sheets |

These permissions are requested only when you use the corresponding feature. You may decline them; however, scanning functionality will be unavailable if camera access is denied.

## Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's standard app storage (AsyncStorage). Data is not backed up to any cloud service by the app. Your device's own backup settings may apply.

## Data Sharing

EthioGrade does not share any data with third parties. The CSV export feature uses Android's built-in Share sheet — you choose where to send it.

## Children's Privacy

EthioGrade is designed for use by teachers, not students. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Updates will be reflected in the app's store listing.
